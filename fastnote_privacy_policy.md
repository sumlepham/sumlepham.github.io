# Privacy Policy — Quick Note

**Effective date:** June 21, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how **Quick Note** (the "App") handles your
information. <!-- TODO: fill in developer/company name --> ("we", "us") provides
the App. Quick Note is designed to be private by default: your content stays on
your device.

## What we collect

**We do not collect, transmit, or sell your personal data.** Quick Note has no
user accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no backend server.

- **Your notes, voice recordings, photos, attachments, reminders, and settings**
  are stored **only on your device** (in the app's local database and file
  storage). We cannot see them and they are never uploaded to us or any third
  party.

## How permissions are used

The App requests the following permissions, each used only for the feature named:

- **Microphone** — to record voice notes. Recordings are saved locally.
- **Speech Recognition (iOS)** — to transcribe your voice notes to text. On
  supported devices this runs **on-device**; audio is not sent to us.
- **Camera** — to take photos for photo notes. Photos are saved locally.
- **Photo Library** — to attach existing images to a note. Selected images are
  copied into the app's local storage only.
- **Notifications & exact alarms (Android)** — to deliver reminder
  notifications at the time you schedule.

## Network use

Quick Note works offline. The **only** time the App uses the internet is to
**download the on-device speech-recognition model the first time you transcribe
a voice note**. This downloads model files from the speech engine's distribution
host; it does **not** upload your recordings, notes, or any personal data.
Transcription itself runs on your device.

## Third-party services

The App does not include advertising, analytics, or crash-reporting SDKs. It uses
an open-source on-device speech-recognition engine (Whisper) and, on supported
iOS versions, Apple's on-device speech recognition. Neither transmits your audio
or notes to us.

## Data sharing and selling

We do **not** sell, rent, or share your data with anyone. Because your content
never leaves your device, there is nothing for us to share.

## Children's privacy

Quick Note is intended for a general and teenage audience. We do not knowingly
collect personal information from children, and the App does not collect personal
information from anyone. If you believe a child has provided personal information
through a misconfiguration, please contact us. <!-- TODO: confirm target age

## Your rights and choices

Because all data is stored locally:

- You can delete any note at any time (deleted notes stay in Trash for 30 days,
  then are permanently removed).
- **Uninstalling the App removes all of its local data from your device.**
